# Privacy Policy — Build Pipeline Idle

**Last updated:** 
22/09/2026

This privacy policy describes how Build Pipeline Idle ("the app", "we",
"our") handles information when you use it.

## Data we do not collect

The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email,
contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information, and does not
include any user-generated content or chat feature. Your game progress
(producers owned, currency, achievements, and preferences) is stored only on
your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.

## Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob, and offers optional rewarded ads (for an
in-game bonus) and a banner ad. To do this, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may
collect and process:

- Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
- Device information (device model, OS version, general device settings)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)
- App interaction data (ads shown, clicked, or completed)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly, and is
used to serve and measure ads, including personalizing them where your
device/region settings allow it. Google's own privacy policy governs this
processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required (for example, in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your
consent before showing personalized ads, via Google's User Messaging
Platform. You can withdraw or change that consent at any time by clearing
the app's data in your device's app settings, which resets the consent
prompt.

## Children

This app is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's
Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Third parties

The only third party this app shares data with is Google, through the
Google Mobile Ads SDK described above. We do not sell any data, and we do
not have our own analytics, crash reporting, or backend server.

## Your choices

- You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's ad settings
  (Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
- You can delete all local app data at any time via Android's
  Settings → Apps → Build Pipeline Idle → Storage → Clear data, or by using
  the in-app "Erase everything" control in Settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL
with a new "Last updated" date.
